---
name: Beginner AI copy insight
description: User-stated concern to address in copy for GreenElephant's beginner AI audience.
---

Some people in the intended audience worry that AI could make them less capable. Copy should show how to learn and use AI while keeping their own judgment and decision-making.

**Why:** The user identified this concern when asking for homepage copy.

**How to apply:** For beginner AI training, frame AI as something the learner can understand and direct, not something that replaces their thinking. Keep the wording simple in both English and French.
