## LENS 1 — INFLUENCE (Red #cc3333)

### Mental Models & Frameworks

**1101 Influence Strategies**  
The three main strategies people use to influence others in communication: domination (using force to win), manipulation (appealing to emotions with a hidden agenda), and conscious influence (being clear with intentions and respecting others).

**1103 GreenBlueRed™**  
A behavioral communication framework and coding language. Blue focuses on informing (focus on self), Green focuses on connecting and empathy (focus on other), and Red focuses on agreeing or common action (shared focus).

**1105 Head-Up Display**  
A mental model and visual lens used to step back, observe, and consciously evaluate the colors and levels of communication as they happen in real-time.

### Red Verbal Techniques (SAY & WRITE)

**1201 Advising** *(Red)*  
A red verbal technique focused on giving constructive feedback and advice to others to drive action.

**1202 Red Question** *(Red)*  
Leading inquiries or interrogations designed to guide or influence the other person towards a specific outcome or decision.

**1203 Suggesting** *(Red)*  
A red verbal technique used for proposing an action, idea, or solution for common agreement.

**1204 Supporting** *(Red)*  
A red verbal technique used to reassure people and redirect their efforts towards a shared goal.

**1205 Ordering** *(Red)*  
A red verbal technique used for giving direct instructions, commands, or mandates.

**1206 Agreeing** *(Red)*  
A red verbal technique used for expressing common grounds, aligning on decisions, and moving forward.

### Red Non-Verbal Behaviours (DO & MOVE)

**1304 Red Rhythm** *(Red)*  
A non-verbal behavior where a collective rhythm or deliberate pause is imposed on the group.

**1305 Red Timing** *(Red)*  
A non-verbal behavior where the timing of communication is dictated by collective needs, schedules, or imposed deadlines.

### Red Intentions (FEEL & INTEND)

**1401 Uniting** *(Red)*  
A red intention focused on connecting individuals and building a group into a cohesive team.

**1402 Seducing (Being Liked)** *(Red)*  
A red intention driven by the desire to be liked, to appeal to others, and to build charisma.

**1403 Fixing Problems** *(Red)*  
A red intention strictly focused on finding solutions, troubleshooting, and answering a brief together.

**1404 Changing Others** *(Red)*  
A red intention aimed at actively influencing, coaching, or modifying other people's behaviors.

**1405 Taking Over** *(Red)*  
A red intention involving the ability and willingness to step in and take control of a meeting, conversation, or process.

## LENS 2 — ATTITUDE (Orange #ff9933)

### Mental Models

**2101 Attitude to Change**  
A mental model describing four attitudes towards unplanned change: resisting (Type 0), doing (Type I), learning (Type II), and actively balancing learning with doing (Type III).

**2103 Micro-Habits**  
Small, conscious communication routines built on a behavioral loop of a trigger, an intention, an action/behavior, and a reward.

**2401 Attitude 0 (Resisting)**  
An attitude that fears and resists change, says no, builds walls, and demonstrates low engagement.

**2402 Attitude I (Doing)**  
An attitude that expects changing the world around you will change your experience; highly focused on problem-solving and outer action.

**2403 Attitude II (Learning)**  
An attitude that expects internal change through learning and reflecting will change the world; driven by inner focus.

**2404 Attitude III (Growing)**  
An attitude that actively and dynamically balances changing the world (doing) with changing oneself (learning).

## LENS 3 — CHAORDIC (Amber #ffcc00)

### Mental Models & Structural Elements

**3101 Chaordic Balance**  
The state of balancing chaos (creative content and ideas) and order (structured process and rules) in a group to prevent wasted time and enable collective intelligence.

**3102 Algorithm Canvas**  
Structured processes and meeting algorithms designed to help groups work together efficiently in collective intelligence.

**3103 Types of Conversation**  
The four depths of communication: small talk/briefing, debates/discussions, negotiation/dialogue, and co-creation/presencing.

**3105 Framing**  
The process of defining the purpose, rules, and timing at the start of a session to establish psychological safety and focus.

**3111 Chaordic Roles**  
Specific, neutral social roles (such as Host, Harvester, Steward) adopted to manage the group process and facilitate collective intelligence.

**3112 Participant / Expert**  
A content-focused role where an individual produces knowledge, shares ideas, and participates in the process based on their expertise.

**3113 Harvester**  
A chaordic process role focused on serving the group by listening, collecting, and crystallizing valuable outputs and collective intelligence.

**3114 Host**  
A chaordic process role focused on serving the group by holding time, maintaining rules, and keeping the psychological space safe without interfering with the content.

**3115 Steward**  
A meta-host role that looks after the long-term processes, rules, values, and structures across multiple meetings or projects.

## LENS 4 — FLOW (Lime-green #cccc33)

### Mental Models

**4101 Measuring Flow**  
The process of tracking the mental state of optimal experience by mapping a person's perceived challenge against their perceived competence.

**4102 Conscious Feedback**  
The interface of giving and receiving information (praise, advice, or opinions) to help individuals learn, adjust their flow state, and improve.

**4103 Motivation**  
The internal drive that acts as the essential prerequisite and "wind force" needed to push an experience into a state of flow.

**4104 Challenge**  
The self-perceived level of difficulty or stress inherent in a task or communication situation.

**4105 Skill (Competence)**  
The self-perceived level of ability, knowledge, or confidence an individual has in a given situation.

## LENS 5 — ALIGNMENT (Forest-green #669966)

### Mental Models

**5101 Alignment**  
The internal congruence between what a person is doing, saying, thinking, and feeling during a conflict or communication.

**5102 Congruence**  
The state where a person's verbal techniques, non-verbal behaviors, and underlying intentions perfectly match and align, building trust.

**5103 Meaning**  
The psychological unlocking factor based on logotherapy, which asserts that the meaning an individual assigns to an event dictates their reality and healing.

**5105 Presencing**  
A deep level of conversation where one holds space for another without judgment, acting as an empathic mirror to help them think out loud and reflect.

### Green Verbal Techniques (SAY & WRITE)

**5201 Positive Phrases** *(Green)*  
A green verbal technique used to share factual praise, give credits of intention, and establish a trusting foundation.

**5202 Green Questions** *(Green)*  
Open-ended questions (like what, how, why, when) used to navigate another's mind and understand them with curiosity and empathy.

**5203 Mirroring** *(Green)*  
A green verbal technique used to reflect the other person's situation, tone, or words back to them to help them feel seen and understood.

**5204 Summarising** *(Green)*  
A green verbal technique where what was said is re-expressed briefly with keywords to align understanding and ensure the speaker feels heard.

**5205 Accusation Audit** *(Green)*  
A green verbal technique that lowers defensiveness by preemptively listing the negative assumptions or complaints the other person might be harboring.

**5206 Unlocking** *(Green)*  
A green verbal technique used in negotiation to open new avenues for problem-solving (e.g., "Under what circumstances would you be prepared to...?").

**5207 Labelling** *(Green)*  
An advanced green verbal technique where the listener reformulates and names the underlying feeling or emotion of the speaker.

**5208 Elephant** *(Green)*  
A green verbal technique that involves voicing an obvious but unspoken collective situation factually, respectfully, and without personal judgment.

### Green Non-Verbal Behaviours (DO & MOVE)

**5301 Green Silence** *(Green)*  
A non-verbal behavior characterized by active listening, where silence is deliberately used to focus entirely on understanding the other person.

**5303 Green Body-Language** *(Green)*  
Open, adaptable, and respectful non-verbal posturing focused on making the other person feel comfortable and safe.

**5304 Green Rhythm** *(Green)*  
A non-verbal behavior where one actively adapts, paces, and reacts to the other person's natural conversational rhythm.

**5305 Green Timing** *(Green)*  
A non-verbal behavior involving choosing a time to communicate that is most considerate, convenient, and suitable for the other person.

### Green Intentions (FEEL & INTEND)

**5402 Respect** *(Green)*  
A green intention centered on being entirely okay with others having different opinions, needs, values, and beliefs without judging them.

**5403 Building Trust** *(Green)*  
A green intention focused deliberately on building bridges, establishing rapport, and creating a safe connection.

**5404 Empathy** *(Green)*  
A green intention focused on putting oneself in the other person's shoes to genuinely understand their emotional and factual experience.

**5405 Curiosity** *(Green)*  
A green intention driven by a yearning to understand, allowing information to come from the other person without imposing one's own assumptions.

**5406 Agape** *(Green)*  
A green intention representing the highest form of selfless, unconditional love and connection without expecting anything in return.

## LENS 6 — NEEDS (Blue-teal #009999)

### Mental Models

**6101 Chakra Needs**  
A mental model that categorizes human needs into an easy-to-navigate hierarchy (physical, abundance, autonomy, connection, expression, meaning, peace).

**6104 Functional Conflicts**  
Disagreements focused on tasks or processes that successfully build trust and collective intelligence, as opposed to dysfunctional conflicts focused on ego.

**6105 Types of Love**  
A mental model utilizing the 8 types of Greek love to more accurately understand the deep drivers and motivations behind human connections.

**6106 Stages of Team**  
A mental model outlining the five developmental stages of team formation: forming, storming, norming, performing, and adjourning.

**6201 Conscious Request**  
The practice of formulating needs into requests that are open, positive, concrete, measurable, realistic, and immediate.

### Greek Love Types (FEEL & INTEND)

**6402 Storge**  
Infinite, familiar love built upon acceptance, deep emotional connection, and long-term mutual respect.

**6403 Mania**  
Manic, obsessive, or codependent love often characterized by possession or jealousy.

**6404 Eros**  
Sensual and passionate love that displays affection, attraction, and seeks sexual or physical fulfillment.

**6405 Philia**  
Friendship or brotherly love based on mutual respect, joint interests, and common values without romantic feelings.

**6406 Ludus**  
Flirtatious, playful, and child-like love that engages in activities to make people feel alive and excited.

## LENS 7 — EGO (Sky-blue #3399cc)

### Mental Models & Barriers

**7101 Ego Triggers**  
Internal emotional reactions that occur when an individual compares themselves to others on a physical, emotional, intellectual, or spiritual level.

**7102 Drama Triangle**  
The Karpman triangle detailing the dysfunctional, conflict-prone relationship roles of victim, savior, and aggressor.

**7103 Expressing** *(non-verbal)*  
The non-verbal behavior of openly displaying one's own emotions, thoughts, and feelings from the heart.

**7104 Consciousness Barrier**  
The internal obstacle of knowing your own values, beliefs, and true intentions before attempting to communicate them to someone else.

**7105 Permission Barrier**  
The obstacle of feeling authorized to communicate, driven by either internal self-worth or external hierarchical authority.

**7106 Sensorial Barrier**  
The obstacle of getting a message heard through the noise of information overload by effectively utilizing multiple human senses.

**7107 Language Barrier**  
The obstacle of being understood across different spoken languages, technical jargon, or differing interpretations of words.

**7108 Tangibility Barrier**  
The obstacle of making communication concrete and memorable enough that the analytical brain can easily grasp and recall the meaning.

**7109 Ego Hats**  
The various unofficial social identities and behavioral roles individuals unconsciously swap between during work communication.

### Ego Roles

**7113 Devil's Advocate**  
An ego role that reveals important assumptions but can create chaos and friction by being overly pessimistic or making wrong assumptions.

**7114 Hero**  
An ego role that bravely solves problems with creative solutions but can waste the group's time by creating imaginary problems to solve.

**7115 Narrator / Joker**  
An ego role that mirrors group insights through storytelling and humor, but can waste time by gossiping or losing focus.

**7116 Hermit / Monk**  
An ego role that lets people speak and asks big-picture questions, but can easily become withdrawn, lazy, or passive.

**7117 Artisan**  
An ego role that helps the group build solutions through detailed dialogue, but can easily get bogged down and stuck in the minutiae.

### Blue Verbal Techniques (SAY & WRITE)

**7201 Informing** *(Blue)*  
A blue verbal technique focused on sharing new facts, knowledge, or information that belongs strictly to the speaker.

**7202 Blue Question** *(Blue)*  
A closed, leading, or opinionated question designed to validate the speaker's own assumptions rather than explore the other's mind.

**7203 Judging** *(Blue)*  
A blue verbal technique used to explicitly share one's own opinions, evaluations, and value-based judgments.

**7204 Projecting** *(Blue)*  
A blue verbal technique that involves planning future actions, forecasting, or projecting one's own thoughts and feelings onto another person.

**7205 Apologising** *(Blue)*  
A blue verbal technique used to take accountability, express regret, and manage conflicts.

**7206 Storytelling** *(Blue)*  
A blue verbal technique that utilizes engaging narratives to cross the tangibility barrier and make information memorable.

### Blue Non-Verbal Behaviours (DO & MOVE)

**7301 Blue Silence** *(Blue)*  
A non-verbal behavior where a person focuses entirely inward, getting lost in their own thoughts or zoning out of the conversation.

**7303 Blue Body-Language** *(Blue)*  
Non-verbal posturing focused entirely on the speaker's own physical comfort, space, and self-expression.

**7304 Blue Rhythm** *(Blue)*  
A non-verbal behavior where a person communicates solely according to their own internal pace and speed, ignoring the group's pace.

**7305 Blue Timing** *(Blue)*  
A non-verbal behavior where a person communicates at a time that suits them personally, regardless of the other person's readiness or convenience.

### Blue Intentions (FEEL & INTEND)

**7401 Learning** *(Blue)*  
A blue intention driven by a self-focused desire to acquire knowledge, understand theories, and grow oneself.

**7402 Pragmatism** *(Blue)*  
A blue intention focused heavily on achieving practical results, efficiency, and getting tasks done logically.

**7403 Philautia** *(Blue)*  
A blue intention representing self-compassion, self-care, and the fulfillment of one's own needs before attempting to help others.

**7404 Ego** *(Blue)*  
A blue intention driven by the internal need to build one's own self-confidence, status, and receive recognition or validation.

**7405 Gratitude** *(Blue)*  
A blue intention focused on internally feeling and openly expressing thankfulness and appreciation.

**7406 Responsibilities** *(Blue)*  
A blue intention where an individual focuses on acknowledging, owning, and facing their own duties and obligations.

### Mental Models

**8101 Relationship Dynamics**  
The measurement of how communication polarity flows between individuals, categorizing relations as vertical, horizontal, or dynamically balanced.

**8103 Yin Yang Polarity**  
The dynamic, shifting balance of leading (Yang) and following (Yin) in healthy interpersonal communication.
